The Gerard and Carolyn Martin Foundation was founded in 2021 after the passing of Carolyn.

Each year we, the surviving children of Gerard and Carolyn Martin, have the privilege of giving to many of our favorite charities.

The foundation was organized for exclusively religious, charitable, educational and scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 or the corresponding provision of any future United States Internal Revenue Law, including, for such purposes, the making of distributions to organizations that qualify as exempt organizations under said Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Service Code of 1986.
